'@nxgt/di': minor
---

Add Scopes and Slots. `lifetime: 'scoped'` makes one value per Scope, and `container.createScope(slots)` creates a Scope: `AsyncDisposable`, resolving everything its Container provides, sharing the singletons, and disposing of only what it made (its scoped values and the transients it resolved) in reverse creation order. `.slot(token)` declares a value that comes from outside, such as the request's principal; `createScope` must be given exactly the declared Slots, keyed by Token name, or it fails to compile. A Captive dependency now fails to compile: a singleton's factory cannot `get` a scoped Token or a Slot ("Token 'x' is scoped, captured by a singleton"), and the Container's own `resolve` takes singletons only. A transient declares `bound: 'singleton' | 'scoped'` (default `'singleton'`), the longest lifetime that may capture it. Disposing of the Container does not dispose of its live Scopes. New errors: `ScopeDisposedError` (`DI_SCOPE_DISPOSED`), `ScopeRequiredError` (`DI_SCOPE_REQUIRED`) and `MissingSlotError` (`DI_SLOT_MISSING`). `Container` now takes three type parameters, `Container<Singletons, Scoped, Slots>`; `Container<{ db: Db }>` still means a Container of singletons only. A factory typed apart as `Factory<Singletons, Scoped, T>` fits any Container that provides at least what it uses, and a factory typed to see more than its Provider is given (a scoped Token from a singleton, a later Token) is refused. `options` may be left out only for a singleton. `createScope` on a disposed Container throws `ContainerDisposedError`.
